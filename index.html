About the author

What started as a challenge from a good friend has turned into a hobby. He loves writing in his spare time and has a unique writing style that incorporates his sense of humor in the story along with some made up words.

Bruce describes his writing as "feel good" and "happily ever after" short stories about people who deserve another chance.

He lives in Durban, South Africa with his wife and 2 kids.
